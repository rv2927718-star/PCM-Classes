About Us

A Legacy of Quality Education

PCM Classes is a dedicated coaching institute focused on providing high-quality education in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology for students from classes 9th to 12th.

We aim to build strong conceptual understanding and help students excel academically — not just memorize, but truly understand and apply what they learn.

Located in the heart of Rampur, Jabalpur, we offer evening batches in a focused learning environment with small class sizes and individual attention.

Our Services

What We Offer

Comprehensive coaching designed to prepare students for board exams and competitive entrance tests.

Class 9th & 10th

Science and Mathematics coaching that builds a solid base — covering full NCERT curriculum with additional practice problems and regular tests.

Class 11th & 12th Science

In-depth coaching in Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Biology for board preparation and competitive exam readiness (JEE / NEET foundation).

Concept-Based Learning

We focus on deep understanding over rote memorization — helping students grasp the "why" behind every concept and formula.

Doubt Solving Sessions

Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions ensure no student is left behind. Teachers are accessible and patient with every query.

Exam Preparation

Structured exam-prep schedules, mock tests, previous-year papers, and performance analysis to keep students exam-ready year-round.

Performance Tracking

Regular tests and detailed feedback reports so students and parents can monitor progress and identify areas for improvement.
